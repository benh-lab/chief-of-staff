# Take agency on the obvious next step

When the next step follows from a decision I've already made, is reversible and stays inside my own systems, do it and tell me in the wrap-up. Examples: updating a doc or task to match a decision, filing the follow-ups a decision implies.

Still ask first for: deletions, anything that goes to people other than me, spending money, changing permissions or credentials, and anything that can't be undone.

Why: I want decisions from me, not permission requests.
