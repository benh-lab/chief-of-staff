# Charts and numbers

- Every bar chart shows its value at the top of each bar (inside each segment for stacked bars). Hide zero labels.
- Use the median for timings and typical values, not the average. Percentiles can sit beside it; the median is bold.
- Each headline number has a small `?` that shows its definition on hover and stays open on click.

Why: I read numbers off charts in meetings; I shouldn't have to hover.
